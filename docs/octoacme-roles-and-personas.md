# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to align scope, milestones, and stakeholder expectations
- Coordinates with Developers and team leads to track delivery progress and remove blockers
- Escalates issues to Business Sponsors or leadership when trade-offs, resourcing, or risk thresholds require decisions

---

## Business Sponsor / Product Owner

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors and Product Owners provide strategic direction, business accountability, and prioritization for the outcomes the team is expected to deliver. They keep the initiative aligned to customer and organizational value.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, priorities, and expected outcomes
- Approve scope changes and trade-offs affecting timeline or investment
- Sponsor cross-functional alignment and executive decision-making
- Review progress against value realization and adoption goals

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative delivers measurable business value
- Align delivery decisions with strategic priorities
- Support timely decisions when risk or scope requires escalation

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates and leadership briefings
- Prioritization reviews and business readiness checkpoints
- Trade-off discussions with Project Managers and Product Managers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate the roadmap and feature priorities
- Relies on Project Managers for reporting cadence, dependency tracking, and execution transparency
- Partners with Developers and stakeholders to clarify business intent and expected outcomes

---

## Delivery Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate execution across teams, dependencies, and planning rhythms so work can move forward predictably. They strengthen the operating model between business priorities and engineering execution.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate cross-team dependencies and sequencing of work
- Monitor delivery health, bottlenecks, and capacity constraints
- Help unblock dependencies across squads or functional groups
- Support planning and forecasting for project milestones

### Goals
- Maintain predictable execution across multiple workstreams
- Reduce friction between teams and stakeholders
- Improve visibility into delivery risk and dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Dependency reviews and cross-team coordination meetings
- Delivery health updates and milestone tracking
- Escalation briefs with Project Managers and leadership

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to maintain plan accuracy and issue visibility
- Works with Developers and team leads to resolve blockers and sequencing issues
- Coordinates with Product Managers to translate roadmap priorities into delivery plans

---

## Project Coordinator / PMO Analyst

### Role Summary
Project Coordinators and PMO Analysts provide operational support for governance, status tracking, and documentation. They help maintain consistency across project artifacts and communication routines.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project schedules, action items, and status trackers
- Prepare reporting packs, meeting notes, and governance artifacts
- Track milestones, decisions, and outstanding risks or actions
- Support project documentation and process consistency

### Goals
- Reduce administrative overhead for project leads
- Improve the consistency and quality of project reporting
- Keep decision records and artifacts easy to find and follow

### Typical Communication
- Status reporting and project review meetings
- Action log updates and issue follow-ups
- Documentation requests and governance check-ins

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports Project Managers by maintaining operational detail and reporting cadence
- Coordinates with Business Sponsors and leadership on recurring updates and key milestones
- Works with Developers and team leads to capture implementation status and dependency updates

---

## Risk and Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Risk and Compliance Leads identify, track, and escalate operational, technical, and governance risks. They help ensure the project remains aligned with company standards, regulatory expectations, and stakeholder commitments.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the project risk register and mitigation plans
- Assess issues that may affect schedule, quality, compliance, or security
- Coordinate escalation paths when risk thresholds are reached
- Partner with leadership on governance and mitigation decisions

### Goals
- Reduce exposure to avoidable project, compliance, and operational risk
- Ensure issues are surfaced early and addressed with clarity
- Maintain alignment with organizational standards and expectations

### Typical Communication
- Risk review meetings and governance forums
- Escalation notices and mitigation status updates
- Follow-up with Project Managers, Sponsors, and technical leads

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Project Managers to monitor risk severity and response plans
- Collaborates with Developers and Product Managers to identify technical or delivery risks early
- Escalates to Business Sponsors when a risk affects scope, timeline, or strategic commitments

---

## Stakeholder Liaison / Change Advocate

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons and Change Advocates keep communication flowing between the project team and broader stakeholders. They help ensure adoption, alignment, and readiness for change as the project progresses.

### Responsibilities
- Manage stakeholder communications and expectation-setting
- Gather feedback from affected groups and relay it to the project team
- Support adoption planning, training, and change readiness activities
- Identify stakeholder concerns that may affect rollout or usage

### Goals
- Increase stakeholder confidence and support
- Improve adoption and business readiness for project outcomes
- Ensure communication is timely, consistent, and action-oriented

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings, check-ins, and readiness reviews
- Change impact communications and feedback loops
- Follow-ups with Product Managers, Project Managers, and leadership

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers to ensure stakeholder expectations reflect the roadmap and outcomes
- Supports Project Managers in communication planning and milestone readiness
- Coordinates with Developers and team leads to translate technical changes into stakeholder-friendly updates

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles create a clearer model for accountability, escalation, and cross-functional collaboration across project work.
